FSAE Scaled Front Wing Validation Test

Model Drawing Examples

This project aims to replicate and measure the coefficients of lift and drag for known wings while enabling accurate real-time testing of new wing models. The setup features a custom-built wind tunnel constructed from aluminum, wood, and glass, with 3D-printed PLA components for structural connections, airflow management, and sensor integration.

  • Validated 2026 front wing concept design with scaled-down 3d printed model- achieved within 1.5% drag error when compared to ANSYS Fluent simulation results.
  • Integrated a Raspberry Pi to control the system, operating load cells (for lift and drag), a Pitot tube (for windspeed), and a servo for adjustable angle of attack. Data is displayed via an LCD, with electronics organized on a custom KiCad PCB.
