NC State

Aerodynamic Wind Tunnel

Raleigh, NC

August 2025 — May 2026

This project aims to replicate and measure the coefficients of lift and drag for known wings while enabling accurate real-time testing of new wing models. The setup features a custom-built wind tunnel constructed from aluminum, wood, and glass, with 3D-printed PLA components for structural connections, airflow management, and sensor integration. A Raspberry Pi controls the system, operating sensors such as load cells (for lift and drag), a Pitot tube (for windspeed), and a servo for adjustable angle of attack. Data is going to be displayed via an LCD and input system, with electronics organized on a custom PCB designed in KiCAD.
